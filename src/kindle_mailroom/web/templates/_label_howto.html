{# Shared "how do I label an email?" explainer. Gmail labels are second nature to some people and completely invisible to others - especially on mobile, where "Label as" is buried in an overflow menu. #}
How do I label an email?
  • Gmail on the web: tick the checkbox next to the email (or open it), then click the Labels icon 🏷 in the toolbar and choose “{{ label }}”. Right-clicking the email and picking Label as works too.
  • Gmail on your phone: open the email, tap the ⋮ menu (top right), tap Label as, and pick “{{ label }}”.
  • Do it automatically: open any email from a newsletter you always want on your Kindle, tap ⋮ → Filter messages like this, then Create filter → Apply the label “{{ label }}”. Every future issue is labelled for you.

Every email carrying the label is sent — read or unread. (You can switch to unread-only in Settings.) Once delivered, the email moves from “{{ label }}” to “{{ sent_label }}”, so the watched label always shows exactly what's still waiting to go out.
