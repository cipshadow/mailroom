# Finding your Kindle address and approving your sender email

Kindle Mailroom emails EPUBs to a special `@kindle.com` address tied to your
Kindle (or Kindle app). Amazon only accepts documents from senders you've
explicitly approved — skip step 2 and your documents vanish silently.

## 1. Find your Kindle's email address

1. Go to [Amazon → Manage Your Content and Devices](https://www.amazon.com/hz/mycd/digital-console/alldevices).
2. Open the **Devices** tab and select your Kindle (or the Kindle app you
   want documents to land on).
3. The page shows an email address like `yourname_AB12CD@kindle.com`. That's
   the address to enter in Kindle Mailroom's setup.

If you use `amazon.co.uk` or another regional site, use that site's version
of the page — addresses aren't portable across regions.

## 2. Approve your Gmail address as a sender

1. On the same Amazon page (or **Account → Preferences → Personal Document
   Settings**), find **Approved Personal Document E-mail List**.
2. Click **Add a new approved e-mail address** and enter the Gmail address
   you connected to Kindle Mailroom (shown on the app's dashboard).
3. Save.

Without this step, Amazon drops the email with no notification — the most
common "it didn't arrive" cause.

## 3. `@kindle.com` vs. `@free.kindle.com`

Documents sent to your `@kindle.com` address deliver over Wi-Fi for free.
Some Kindle models also offer a `@free.kindle.com` variant for Wi-Fi-only
delivery at no charge.

Kindle Mailroom accepts either form wherever you enter a Kindle address (the
setup wizard and Settings), so use whichever Amazon shows you — just make sure
the sender approval in step 2 covers the address you pick.
